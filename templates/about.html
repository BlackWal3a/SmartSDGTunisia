About Us

Introduction

This platform was developed as part of a university initiative to address pressing environmental challenges in Tunisia, with a special focus on the Ain Draham forest ecosystem. Our goal is to provide a powerful, data-driven tool for students, researchers, and policymakers to monitor, analyze, and understand our natural environment. By leveraging cutting-edge satellite technology and GIS capabilities, we aim to support sustainable land management and conservation efforts.

Mission Statement

“To provide students, researchers, and institutions with accessible tools to analyze, visualize, and understand environmental data.”

What the Platform Offers

  • Interactive mapping and visualization of geospatial data.

  • Historical trends for forest cover, fire risk, and land use.
