Methods & Documentation

Data Sources

Our platform utilizes data from a variety of sources, including Sentinel, Landsat, and Global Forest Change. These datasets provide global coverage and are updated regularly, allowing us to provide the most accurate and up-to-date information possible.

Preprocessing

All of our data is preprocessed to ensure its quality and consistency. This includes cloud masking, mosaicking, and other techniques to remove atmospheric effects and other sources of noise.

Algorithms

We use a variety of algorithms to analyze our data, including NDVI, fire detection, and terrain models. These algorithms are based on the latest scientific research and are constantly being improved and updated.

Limitations

While we strive to provide the most accurate and up-to-date information possible, there are some limitations to our data and analysis. For example, our data may not be available for all areas, and our algorithms may not be able to detect all types of environmental change.

Update frequency

Our data is updated on a regular basis, but the frequency of updates varies depending on the dataset. For example, our fire data is updated daily, while our forest cover data is updated annually.
